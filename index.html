Carter’s Lullaby

Your voices, your words, your music, for every night and morning.

Your voices

Pick or write the words, then record them in your own voice. Edit any line to say it your way.

Record on your own phone whenever you like — even from bed at 2 a.m. Tap Send my voices and share the little file it makes to the other phone (text it, AirDrop it, anything). On the phone that plays by Carter’s crib, tap Add voices I received and pick that file, just once — from then on, Carter hears Mommy and Daddy all night. The same file also loads Carter’s own crib player — their words, voices, sounds and settings, all of it.

Play

Choose the sound, the volume, and how long it plays. Recorded lines repeat softly with quiet pauses between them.

Matching the live weather works once the app has its own web address. In this preview, choose the weather by hand.

Volume stops at a safe level on purpose. Place the speaker across the room, not next to the crib.
